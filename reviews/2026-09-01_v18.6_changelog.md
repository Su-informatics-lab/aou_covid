## B. Method corrections (the computation, not the transcription)

**B1. The 1.31 "predicted probability" was not a probability.**
`02_models.R` applies `1/(1+exp(-lp))` to a `clogit` linear predictor, which is centred
on the covariate means and carries no intercept. The stored 0.438 / 0.573 are artifacts
of that centring. Replaced throughout with the identified quantity — the profile odds
ratio, **1.78** = 1.33 × 1.18 × 1.13 — and the Methods now state that absolute
probabilities are not estimable from a conditional logistic model.
The Discussion argument built on 1.31 ("more modest than multiplying separate AORs")
is replaced by the defensible comparison: joint product **1.78** against domain-specific
product **2.79**.
**`02_models.R` still contains the faulty calculation and must be corrected.**

**B2. Non-collapsibility caveat moved to where the estimator is defined**, so the
change-in-estimate percentages and the caveat no longer sit five pages apart.
